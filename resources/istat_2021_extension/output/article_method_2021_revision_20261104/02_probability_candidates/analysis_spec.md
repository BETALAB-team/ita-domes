# Probability regression revision: design and source audit

The frozen 52,617 eligible household labels and their 46,712-record dwelling subset remain unchanged. All work writes into this new candidate directory; previous results and 2013 sources stay intact.

The actual Figure 6 graphic has dwelling AREA on the horizontal axis and construction-vintage curves. Its caption describes the reverse orientation. The graphic was inspected directly; its plotted orientation guides this revision. Saved `probablities_only.csv`, the input read by original `05_03_PFE_Analysis.py`, has 350 pooled windows and 125 after count>=5, age<150 and area<150. The read-only historical generator recovered from `e7094f4:resources/istat_data/classification_analysis_bayes.py` uses age centers 15..135 by 5, area centers 40..352 by 24, and inclusive half-windows 15 years/40 m2. This differs materially from the later pfe_pipeline helper and paper prose used in the previous 21-node reconstruction. The root cause was the wrong empirical regression geometry, not evidence that 2021 probabilities should be flat with area.

A first coarse-grid candidate comparison was completed before this archived geometry was recovered. It is preserved under `coarse21_diagnostic` and is not the selected analysis. After source recovery, all candidates and comparisons were rerun on historical geometry. The primary dataset now has 125 supported window centers: 25 ages by 5 areas (40,64,88,112,136). Broad overlapping windows smooth the estimand and reuse households; 125 windows are not 125 independent observations.

Five 70/30 household splits use seeds 11,42,101,2021,2026. Split households BEFORE building window outcomes and fitting regression coefficients; recompute training and test proportions independently. Keep fixed demographic labels. Evaluate identical heldout supported windows, count>=5. Report equal-window RMSE/MAE, count-weighted RMSE, individual range violations and sum-to-one errors. Primary area<150 and all-supported-area targets remain separate. Repeated splits are conditional probability-stage comparisons, not independent replicates, a nested final untouched test or external end-to-end validation.

Candidates are original 05_03 restricted basis [1,age,area,age²]; current backward p-value elimination from the six-term quadratic; printed paper group-specific term masks; the full six-term quadratic jointly solving all four probability responses without elimination; and a simple joint multinomial quadratic in three log odds versus A4. Softmax uses equal-window crossentropy and fixed ridge1e-4 on nonintercepts, without tuning on test splits. Each candidate is fitted on primary or all-supported areas. The low-order probability matrix is preferred when predictive performance supports it; no spline complexity or visual selection is required.

The fair comparison additionally refits the OLD 21-window backward model on each training household split and evaluates it against the SAME NEW 125-node heldout proportions as the new model. This separates improvement against a common target from merely comparing the residuals of two differently smoothed empirical grids. The frozen all-data old model is never evaluated on its own training households as purported holdout evidence.

Original-style vintage profiles now average over historical age-window CENTER nodes: before1950,1950–1969,1970–1989 and1990onward, relative to2021. Empirical points and fitted points use exactly the same equal weights on the same age-center nodes within each vintage at each area. Continuous curves use the same fixed nodes. A window's households may span vintage boundaries; the labels identify the window-center era, not strict household construction-era prevalence. The 2021 pre1950 release cannot identify the paper's before1945 split without extra assumptions. Overlapping window-count sums are explicitly not independent household denominators.

The selected full quadratic has basis order [1,age/100,area/100,age*area/10000,age²/10000,area²/10000] identically in coefficient CSV and predictions. Its validated interpolation rectangle is age15..135 years and area40..136 m2, for the smoothed window estimand. Outside predictions must be flagged; extrapolation is not validated. Selection remains conditional on fixed labels and existing age/area imputations.
