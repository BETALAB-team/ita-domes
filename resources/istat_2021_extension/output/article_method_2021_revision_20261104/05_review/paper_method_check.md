**Energy grouping.** The paper's Algorithm 2 starts with combinations of factor levels. It tests all group pairs, merges the least-separated pair when the adjusted p exceeds the threshold and repeats until all remaining groups differ at that threshold. The new 2021 strict 9H/13E solution already satisfies this stopping criterion. In a larger sample, smaller differences can meet a fixed significance cutoff. The observed difference in group counts can also reflect outcome variance, sample composition, available dwelling categories, and differences in means. No single mechanism was isolated.
