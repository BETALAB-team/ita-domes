# Independent scientific review rubric

Read the complete current claim manuscript and open primary evidence. Check every central claim, rather than accepting source or claim ledgers as proof. Recompute key quantities where feasible. Record unperformed checks, inaccessible sources, and limitations of the review.

Every round covers: source-to-claim correspondence; closest literature and provisional novelty; extension beyond CLIMA; methods/estimands/inference; numerical provenance and denominators; EUReCA validity and conference departures; validation independence; uncertainty and overstatement.

Round emphasis: 01 source accuracy/scope/gap; 02 methods/comparability/reproducibility; 03 Padova/climate/validation/uncertainty; 04 entire scientific argument and every central result/contribution.

For each finding provide ID, severity (critical/major/minor), claim IDs, exact evidence location, consequence, and a checkable remedy. Do not invent issues or remove valid limitations. Distinguish a factual error from missing data that is already explicitly pending.

Report input draft filename and SHA-256, inspected evidence and calculations, substantive findings, and gate recommendation. Save independent critique before receiving previous reviews or author responses. A second pass may then assess earlier closure.

A full cycle requires a saved review, saved author response, new immutable revision, and updated issue register. Four reviews of the same version are not four cycles. Review is not empirical validation.

After round 4, inspect v04 closure of critical/major findings. The prose-stage gate requires at least four full cycles and no critical factual/methodological error presented as settled. Planned/missing results remain visibly incomplete.
