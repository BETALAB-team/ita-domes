## Evidence and method fixed before new annual runs

The [EnergyPlus EPW data dictionary](https://bigladdersoftware.com/epx/docs/24-2/auxiliary-programs/energyplus-weather-file-epw-data-dictionary.html) identifies opaque cover 99 and horizontal-IR 9999 as missing codes; cloud cover is used only when infrared is unavailable. The current [EnergyPlus climate reference](https://energyplus.readthedocs.io/en/v26.2.0/guides/engineering-reference/5.1-climate-calculations.html) defines effective radiative sky temperature by IR_H = σ Tsky⁴, where IR_H is downwelling horizontal infrared rate and σ = 5.6697×10⁻⁸ W m⁻² K⁻⁴. The [JRC PVGIS TMY documentation](https://joint-research-centre.ec.europa.eu/photovoltaic-geographical-information-system-pvgis/using-pvgis-5/pvgis-5-tools/pvgis-typical-meteorological-year-tmy-generator_en) describes IR(h) and the EPW/local-hour-ending export convention. Sources accessed 20 October 2026; these are implementation documentation, not empirical validation sources.

Use the existing valid horizontal IR in EPW zero-based column 12. Divide its Wh/m² by the one-hour interval to obtain W/m²; derive Tsky_C = (IR_H/σ)^0.25−273.15 for each record. Replace only `weather.general_data['average_dt_air_sky']` with mean(Tair−Tsky), before any zone load preprocessing. Retain EUReCA's native annual-mean longwave approximation, its surface radiative coefficients and all physical/scenario parameters. No cloud code is clipped, filled or interpreted as clear sky. Do not replace model humidity, solar or other data silently. Preserve the raw EPW/JSON and save hourly derived sky values, old/new scalar, checks and hashes. The single-commit sensible dispatcher correction remains unchanged.

## Field and time audit

`audit_weather_forcing_v4.py` inventories dry bulb, dew point, RH, pressure, IR, GHI/DNI/DHI, wind direction/speed and sky cover, with finite/range/sentinel counts. IR, temperature, RH, pressure and solar fields match their corresponding JSON rows exactly; EPW wind is rounded to one decimal. Each exported EPW year/month/day/hour−1 label matches the corresponding JSON label; this is a record-pair check, not a claim that the JSON UTC label and pvlib's local interval-start label are identical instants. pvlib labels EPW hour H as H−1 at fixed UTC+1. Pair IR and air from the same EPW record, without interpolation or a new clock shift. The annual mean is unaffected by a common row permutation, but metadata and the precise convention remain recorded.

JSON irradiance time offsets are about +0.17 h; EPW comments report about −0.83 h. Native EUReCA computes solar position at the pvlib index and does not apply that offset. This approximation is retained and explicitly limits hourly solar/peak fidelity; no exact irradiance phase or measured hourly accuracy is claimed. Do not infer an unverified UTC correction from those offset labels alone. No new annual solar calculation or weather-year matching is introduced.

The stricter whole-file audit initially failed at Palermo row 3631: wind −0.1 m/s in EPW, matching JSON −0.09 m/s at label 20140601:0700 (EPW hour-ending 8). That is a genuinely invalid source wind, not merely valid rounding. Preserve it unchanged and retain the failed logs. This bounded 1C path has fixed ACH and no natural/mechanical ventilation or PV; source searches show wind reads only in the inactive natural-ventilation branch. Thus the final gate explicitly covers active forcing, while this loaded-but-unused wind record remains a failed weather-variable QC item. It must not support general weather-quality, wind or ventilation claims. The local dispatcher guards against enabling natural/mechanical ventilation; future workflows that use wind must resolve the source record first. All other loaded field checks and the JSON mapping remain mandatory. No validity range is widened to hide this exception.

## Gates, chronology and count

Before runs, require finite nonmissing IR >0, valid active forcing, complete 8,760-record mapping, σTsky⁴ reconstruction error <1e-9 W/m², broad effective-sky range −100…70°C and annual mean air-minus-sky within 0…50 K. Check that the opaque-surface longwave term is now a loss with the expected coefficient times the positive scalar; save an independent recomputation from zone forcing. Do not use the broad screens as empirical validation.

Freeze this protocol, correction code, unchanged single-commit module, source/config/design and weather/audit hashes before each run. First execute the five corrected Padova cases and pass full hourly air/surface/mass residuals ≤1e-6 W, exact old-state continuity, transition tests, source schedule mapping, repeatability, setpoint, calendar, zero-cooling and warm-up gates. Only then run five cases each at the three other coordinates. There will be 20 v4 annual runs: twelve primary cases plus four repeat and four longer-warm-up checks. No calibration, scenario change, new weather acquisition or external checkout/environment mutation is permitted. Recompute every scientific summary and post-hoc partition from v4 outputs; keep all prior outputs quarantined. Independent review and empirical validation remain separate requirements.
