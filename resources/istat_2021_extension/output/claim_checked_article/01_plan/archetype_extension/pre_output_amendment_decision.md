# Pre-output design amendment: conditional shared-profile ensembles

20 October 2026. This is a transparent amendment after input selection and before any new annual thermal outcome. It is not represented as the untouched original protocol.

The v1 input rule selected different household IDs for the four group prototype roles, but all five group/pooled prototype roles have the same complete temperature-profile hash. The methods owner and fresh pre-run Astra auditor independently confirmed this. Under fixed physics, their simulated predictions and paired deviations would therefore be identical by construction. V1 protocol, input selection, manifests and the saved audit remain unchanged; no v1 annual simulation was executed. This is an informative input-representation collapse, not a hidden failed energy campaign.

Root selects the candidate v2 discussed with both methods and auditor. Keep the exact32held-out respondents and13,592training records. Within each of the four observed source-control branches, select one real pooled training-input medoid by the declared input-space rule. Estimate branch probabilities from training counts, once pooled and separately within each archived demographic archetype. Run each real component independently. The conditional archetype representation changes its mixture probabilities; it does not invent a demographic temperature trajectory, occupancy curve or gain schedule.

For each weather exposure and endpointY, pooled prediction is Σ_b P_train(b)Y_b, and archetype prediction is Σ_b P_train(b|g)Y_b. The actual held-out detailed-input model outputs remain references for model-input compression fidelity, not observed truth. The two representations share all four component profiles, code and physical/weather assumptions. This removes a difference in component-profile count. Conditional probabilities still add model parameters:12free probabilities across four groups versus3pooled. No full statistical-complexity matching, demographic causal effect or general algorithm superiority is claimed.

Retain annual useful sensible heating and the individual annual maximum as endpoints. The mixture's peak prediction is the weighted mean of the component individual annual maxima, not the maximum of an averaged hourly load curve, coincident district demand or an equipment-sizing value. Primary loss is stratification-reweighted squared deviation/RMSE; signed and absolute deviations are secondary descriptive quantities. These choices precede all new thermal outcomes and do not depend on whether the conditional representation looks better.

The held-out selection, its group×branch inclusion weights, source-control semantics, common physical dwelling/gains/season and four coordinate TMYs remain fixed. No extra group×branch prototype factorial is selected.
