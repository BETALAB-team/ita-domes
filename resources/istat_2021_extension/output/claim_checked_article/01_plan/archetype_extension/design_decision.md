# Design selection — pending pre-run launch gate

20 October 2026. Root reconciles the evidence and methods recommendations; no new annual outcome has been examined at this decision.

The implementation uses the four archived demographic group assignments in lowercase `clusters.csv`, joined to actual 2013 survey responses. The evidence owner found agreement with published group shares and most demographic table cells, while exact published-branch membership identity remains unestablished. Uppercase `Clustered.csv`, the current20-cluster exploratory script and the later seeded four-cluster reconstruction are different artifacts. The primary assignment must be described as archived and article-consistent, without claiming an exact executable reproduction of the published analysis.

The primary extension asks: **For a fixed illustrative dwelling, how much within-model annual-heating and hourly-maximum representation deviation arises when detailed held-out household operational inputs are replaced by one archetype-specific real-input prototype, compared with a single pooled prototype?** The four coordinate TMYs provide controlled weather exposures. The previous placement experiment remains linked secondary evidence; new household cases are not multiplied by every placement alternative.

Selected experiment: option2 in `03_methods/archetype_extension/bridge_options.md`, refined to two held-out respondents per demographic group × four supported source-control branches (32 test IDs). Selection is random without replacement with seed20261020 before profile/prototype evaluation. The published study is unweighted. The exact eligible domain and branch counts must be frozen, excluding unsupported/missing operational data and documenting main-system coding. The official survey weight is not treated as a population/design weight here.

Each group's prototype is a real training respondent minimizing squared distance to that group's training24-hour temperature-input centroid, with smallest-ID ties. The pooled prototype uses the same rule on all training inputs. Test respondents are excluded before either selection. Only input vectors select prototypes; energy outputs do not fit, choose or tune them. Averaging fractional operating hours into a fabricated schedule is avoided. Identical complete profiles may share a thermal execution if role-to-profile hashes remain explicit.

Annual useful sensible heating and maximum hourly sensible load remain primary endpoints. Signed/absolute group-prototype and pooled-prototype deviations use each held-out detailed-input simulation as a within-model reference. Descriptive cohort metrics are weighted to the defined eligible unweighted group×branch shares, with exact cell counts and normalization. This is not measured prediction accuracy or a national population estimate. With two test cases per cell, uncertainty and sample sensitivity are limited; no unjustified confidence interval or stable population percentile is reported.

One assumed dwelling/envelope, gains, infiltration, ideal sensible energy boundary and common season remain fixed. Joint survey heating-hour/control inputs and source-ID placement differ. Demographic labels do not create temperatures, occupancy curves, internal gains or a causal demographic effect. This implements the operational-input component of the user archetypes; it does not implement the published dwelling-allocation polynomial or claim a locally representative stock. Source-control fixtures must cover all four branches before execution.

The four-group-versus-one-pooled comparison is not complexity matched. Any lower or higher deviation is a result for these representations; it cannot establish a general advantage of demographic grouping independently of prototype count. A mixture identity is not used as evidence of benefit. Further equal-capacity or larger-cohort tests require a scientific reason and a separate design, rather than being added after seeing a favorable or unfavorable result.
