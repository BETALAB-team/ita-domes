# Archetype extension validation matrix: executed v2 is authoritative

This is the current validation matrix for the executed archetype_campaign_v2. The governing design is mixture_protocol_v2.md and its immutable pre-run copy in 05_simulations/archetype_campaign_v2/pre_run/selection_protocol.md. The table below supersedes the retained historical v1 table. Frozen run files, protocols, source snapshots and primary result tables remain unchanged.

Two representations share four real source-control components. Their distinction is pooled versus demographic-group-conditioned training probabilities, with three versus 12 free probability parameters. The predeclared endpoints are annual useful sensible space-heating demand and the expected individual annual maximum hourly heating load under each mixture. The latter averages separately calculated individual maxima; it is not the maximum of an averaged load curve or a coincident urban peak.
