# Archetype bridge options — preparatory, not yet a frozen experiment

The four demographic groups must index real survey-conditioned inputs. They do not imply four physical dwellings or four measured operating schedules. The strongest assignment lead is lowercase `clusters.csv`, with four archived labels 0–3, not uppercase `Clustered.csv` (five saved labels), the current exploratory script (20 clusters), or the later reconstructed baseline. Context verification is required before its primary use; exact published-branch identity is not established. The published analysis is unweighted, so the extension can use a clearly defined unweighted empirical domain without claiming national population estimates.

## Feasible comparisons

1. **Group distribution illustration.** Draw eight eligible real respondents per group with a frozen random seed. Run the same dwelling under their own source-mapped heating inputs at the four already selected coordinate TMYs. Report within-group spread and group distributions. A pooled mixture is a descriptive reference only: equality between a mixture of group means and the pooled mean is bookkeeping, not evidence that archetypes improve anything. Maximum 128 scientific case roles before profile deduplication, plus checks.
2. **Held-out input-compression comparison (recommended).** Hold out eight eligible respondents per group before selecting profiles. Select one real input medoid per demographic group from the remaining training inputs, and one pooled training-input medoid, without using any new energy outcome. Compare each held-out respondent's detailed-input EUReCA output with its group-medoid prediction and the same pooled-medoid prediction. This gives a non-tautological comparison of four demographic-conditioned representative inputs versus one pooled representative input. Maximum 37 profiles × four climates = 148 annual scientific case roles, plus approximately eight repeat/warm-up checks. Duplicate complete temperature profiles may share an explicitly logged thermal execution. This is model-input compression fidelity, not measured prediction accuracy; a four-versus-one capacity difference must remain explicit.
3. **Larger held-out cohort.** Sixteen respondents per group give at most 69 profiles × four climates = 276 case roles before checks/deduplication. This better describes the evaluation cohort but does not by itself establish national representativeness, empirical accuracy or optimal sample size. Expand only if the question and runtime budget justify it, not because the smaller cohort's results are inconvenient.

## Recommended provisional specification for option 2

- Domain: archived four-group membership joined uniquely to 2013 survey IDs; valid integer 0–8 hours in all three blocks; central/individual heating (`q_3_0` 1/2); positive reported total block hours. Record exclusions and support by group. Confirm applicability/coding against primary survey evidence before freeze.
- Selection: uniform without replacement within each eligible group, seed 20261020; eight test respondents per group. Use all remaining eligible records for input-only medoid selection. No output-driven ID choice or tuning.
- Input medoid: construct the actual source rule's 24-hour heating temperature vector for each training respondent, including its source ID seed and respondent-conditioned control/fireplace branch. Select the real respondent closest in squared Euclidean temperature distance to the training input centroid; deterministic smallest-ID tie break. This is an input-space medoid under squared distance, not a simulated group mean, a fractional-hour schedule or an identified published archetype schedule.
- Comparators: pooled medoid selected by the same rule from all training records; four group medoids selected within their respective training memberships. Test households do not enter either profile selection. No regression fitting, clustering rerun, calibration or comparison to measured truth.
- Outcomes: retain annual useful sensible heating and maximum hourly sensible heating load. For each held-out household/site compute signed and absolute compression errors for group-medoid and pooled-medoid predictions, and their paired difference. Report each group's test distribution, range/SD and individual errors. Eight cases per group is a small illustrative holdout; do not present stable population quantiles or survey-design confidence intervals. Pooled cohort metrics must state whether groups are equally weighted or reweighted to the defined eligible empirical group shares.
- Attribution controls: same 80 m² dwelling/envelope, constant internal gains, infiltration, equipment boundary, source season and weather conventions as reviewed v5. Demographic age/education/household-size labels never become temperature or gain values automatically. Only actual heating/control responses differ. Thus demographic grouping conditions operational responses while physical-building and internal-gain effects are intentionally held fixed.
- Placement: retain the source-seeded reconstruction as the primary bridge. The completed v5 early/late experiment remains a linked timing-ambiguity study, rather than multiplying every new respondent by all placement alternatives. A new representative placement sensitivity would need a separate predeclared question.
- Inference: a detailed-input model output is a reference for representation loss, not ground truth. Lower compression error cannot establish actual household accuracy or savings. Published demographic grouping and this later operational bridge must remain distinct. The four-versus-one representation comparison is not a complexity-matched claim of algorithm superiority.

No annual run is authorized by this options document. The orchestrator must reconcile assignment provenance, domain, sample size and estimand, then freeze the protocol and profile manifest before new outcomes are inspected.
