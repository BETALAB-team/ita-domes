The pooled representation uses training branch probabilities P_train(b)=N_train,b/N_train. The archetype-conditioned representation uses P_train(b|g)=N_train,g,b/N_train,g from the actual archived demographic group membership. ## Endpoints, losses and weights fixed before outputs

Endpoints are annual useful sensible space-heating demand(kWh) and individual annual maximum hourly sensible heating load(kW). Each held-out respondent is simulated with its complete source-conditioned input to provide the detailed-input model reference y_i,s. This is a within-model reference, not measured ground truth.

Primary evaluation per site and endpoint is descriptive stratification-weighted squared deviation and its square root(RMSE) for the conditional and pooled endpoint predictions, plus paired squared-deviation difference: sum_i w_i[(p_g(i),s−y_i,s)^2−(p_pool,s−y_i,s)^2]. Negative favors conditional representation within this frozen experiment. There is no outcome tuning. RMSE is the square root of sum_i w_i(p−y)^2; weights sum to one. Secondary descriptive statistics are weighted signed and absolute deviations, group-specific RMSE using within-group weights, all32 individual detailed outcomes/predictions and weighted group spread. A conditional-mean prediction motivates squared loss; this change from v1 MAE emphasis is explicitly pre-output.

For original eligible stratum(g,b) size N_gb and group size N_g, each of its two test records has inclusion probability2/N_gb, overall empirical-domain evaluation weight w_i=N_gb/(2×13624), and within-group weight N_gb/(2N_g). These are evaluation stratification weights, distinct from training mixture probabilities. They do not imply national population inference, unbiased national prediction error, survey-design confidence intervals or model-validation accuracy. Training depends on the split. No percentage or relative loss is primary: all endpoint deviations retain kWh/kW, MSE retains squared units, RMSE retains endpoint units. Therefore no reference-value division or zero-denominator replacement is required. If a detailed endpoint is zero, its absolute/squared deviation is calculated normally; do not suppress it.
