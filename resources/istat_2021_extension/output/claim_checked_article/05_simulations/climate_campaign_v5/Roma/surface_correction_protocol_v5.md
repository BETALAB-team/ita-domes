The zero-glazed roof has no such attribute and therefore reuses the preceding wall's 19.2 m² opaque area and wall-plane irradiation instead of its own 80 m² area and roof-plane irradiation. Both shortwave and longwave forcing are affected. An actual-source trace at line 839 confirms this. Changing the expected coefficient to match the wrong execution is forbidden. All 66 earlier annual executions (including the separate v4 replay) are quarantined for energy-effect claims.

The local `surface_forcing_1c.py` explicitly initializes each external wall/roof's own irradiance, opaque/glazed area, construction U_net, absorptance, Rse, radiative coefficient and sky-view factor. Opaque shortwave is shading×global_plane×absorptance×Rse×U_net×opaque_area; opaque longwave is −sky_factor×Rse×U_net×opaque_area×h_r×mean_air_minus_sky.
