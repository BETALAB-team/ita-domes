## Primary source and interpretation

Read the primary paper text for the demographic inputs, equal-family weighting and future simulation scope; visually inspected primary page 7 (Table 3, Figure 5 and explicit unweighted statement). Compared the full source-control functions in `C:/Works/GRINS/GRINS-Tool/classes/EndUse.py` directly. The archived group's aggregate correspondence supports the qualified article-consistent use; it cannot establish identity to the inaccessible published remote branch. This audit does not independently certify every article cell or reproduce all demographic preprocessing.

The source repeats a respondent's ID-seeded contiguous schedule every active day, resets the same seed separately in each eight-hour band, and uses 22/11, 22/19, 24/13 or 24/21 °C. That creates correlated placement across equal-length bands and is an inherited modelling rule, not observed occupancy or temperatures. The source uses climate E's common season; four coordinate TMY exposures do not make the selected respondents residents of those cities. Heating-hour answers, control equipment and fireplace indicators are used as operational inputs; demographic labels never manufacture gains or presence.

The requested v1 squared-distance medoid is mathematically equivalent to the training member minimizing the sum of squared Euclidean distances. It is not the unsquared-distance k-medoids objective. Selection near a mixed-temperature centroid can choose a low-duration, mild-setback schedule such as the observed 0/2/0 medoid without making it representative of actual population operation. The frozen definition is coherent, but this behavioral limitation and the observed collapse must be visible.

The protocol properly distinguishes useful sensible thermal energy from fuel/final energy, model-reference fidelity from measurements, descriptive small-holdout results from population accuracy, and nominal four-versus-one capacity from an equal-capacity causal test. With the realized collapse, actual prototype capacity is equal at one. Absence of measured validation is a stated scope limit, not a demand to collect unavailable measurements before this bounded computational study.

## Candidate v2 advice — not an audit approval

Root proposes the same four global training branch profiles in both representations, with pooled training branch probabilities versus training probabilities conditional on demographic group. This is a coherent distinct question: does group information improve a model-based conditional ensemble prediction at fixed component capacity? It makes group information enter the weighted ensemble, without pretending that demographic labels create physical individual schedules. The same 32 held-out IDs must remain excluded from medoids and all mixture probabilities. Both representations must use exactly the same component endpoints and no held-out branch information to predict individual test respondents.

For either annual heating or annual maximum, define the prediction explicitly as `sum_b P_train(b|group)*endpoint_b`, compared with `sum_b P_train(b)*endpoint_b`. For annual maximum this is the mean of component maxima; it is not the maximum of the averaged hourly curve, which would be a different coincident-aggregate estimand. Mean predictors naturally target squared loss; MAE remains a valid descriptive comparison but does not make these optimal absolute-loss estimators. Freeze endpoints, loss formulas, weighting and all component choices before thermal results, retain v1 collapse evidence, and document why the amendment follows the user's implementation objective. Exact v2 code/manifest audit remains outstanding.
