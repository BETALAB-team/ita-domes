Expected individual annual maximum is explicitly a probability-weighted mean of separately simulated maxima, not the maximum of an averaged curve or a coincident aggregate peak.

The v1 collapse remains an input-only result and does not prove general absence of demographic information. V2 is transparently a post-input/pre-output change, not the unchanged original hypothesis. The computational reference is detailed-input EUReCA, not measurements; unavailable measured data does not bar this disclosed experiment.
