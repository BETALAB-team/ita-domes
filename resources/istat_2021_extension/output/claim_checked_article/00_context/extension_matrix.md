## Selected inference boundary

The unit is a **documented illustrative dwelling under a fixed survey-derived operating-hours constraint**,exposed first to Padova weather. The selected survey record does not make it city-representative. Within-block placement is a modelling assumption;holding daily/block sums fixed isolates its conditional model effect. The source reconstruction is already seeded contiguous;the alternatives must not be described as contrasting contiguous with scattered schedules. Equal block hours do not guarantee equal heat input or comfort,so temperature/energy checks belong to the simulation protocol.

The result can be a model-response sensitivity statement. It cannot establish observed behaviour,true timing,improved estimation,household accuracy,causal behaviour effects,national energy savings,or universal climate transferability. Primary novelty wording must remain a hypothesis until the closest literature is reviewed. The chapter's aggregate agreement is contextual motivation,not this new experiment's empirical validation.
