Independent schedule benchmark

45 cases. Reference: Milano__Multi-family__P2.

Schedule evidence: synthetic. Predeclared Energy Studio synthetic stress-test cases; no ISTAT inputs.

EnergyPlus receives independent schedules. The known-aggregate hybrid receives only household size, annual appliance kWh, mean presence and equivalent thermostat hours. The ISTAT-profile hybrid receives demographic assignments and frozen survey indices. Both use only one reference EP result. No target thermal solver, 1R1C state or hourly hybrid prediction is used.

Target EP outputs are evaluation only. Geometry and weather are shared experimental controls. This is a model-to-model stress test, not measured-building validation. Synthetic household cases are not a representative population sample.
