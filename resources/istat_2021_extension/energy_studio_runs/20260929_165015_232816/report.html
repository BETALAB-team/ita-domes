One EnergyPlus reference + ISTAT transfer

36 scenarios · 100 m² · reference: Palermo__Multi-family__A2

The three methods, simply

  1. EnergyPlus: build and simulate a virtual home for each case.
  2. Hybrid: simulate one home. Ask how the survey indices differ between the new household/context and the reference; multiply the reference annual energy by that ratio. No second thermal calculator.
  3. ISTAT only: report what the survey analysis supports: heating/cooling hours and reconstructed appliance electricity. Thermal kWh remain blank.

Transfer process

E(target,u) = E_EP(reference,u) × I_ISTAT(target,u) / I_ISTAT(reference,u)

  1. Fit supported survey groups on training households. Pool predictions within each region × dwelling × archetype cohort; cooling uses valid reported hours.
  2. Run the reference EnergyPlus case once.
  3. Calculate separate heating, cooling and appliance ratios. Save predictions before any target simulations.
  4. Optionally run target EnergyPlus cases to assess error, excluding the reference. Never feed these results back into predictions.

Heating and cooling hours are proxies, not measured annual thermal energy. Their proportional scaling is a testable assumption. No hybrid hourly/monthly load shapes are invented. Missing/unsupported indices and zero denominators are flagged in the audit.

Assumptions and limitations

  • One fixed-area hypothetical space; not a surveyed home or a metered validation case.
  • Thermal transfer assumes annual demand scales with reported daily hours. This is an empirical hypothesis, not a physical identity.
  • Survey indices use training households, supported joint cohorts and survey weights. Missing cooling hours are excluded, not filled.
  • Cooling hours describe reporting cooling users; they are not an unconditional population cooling-energy index.
  • Survey macro-regions do not identify city weather. Hours do not identify season length or degree days.
  • Hybrid cannot resolve changes in orientation, U-values or exposure within the same survey category; no target thermal solver is used.
  • Hybrid annual estimates have no inferred hourly shape, peak load or monthly distribution.
  • Detailed EP models use assumed daily schedules, adiabatic neighbours and constant ground temperature.
  • Appliance input and transfer use the same survey reconstruction; agreement is not independent validation.
  • Data-only reports hours and reconstructed appliance kWh; thermal kWh remain unavailable.
