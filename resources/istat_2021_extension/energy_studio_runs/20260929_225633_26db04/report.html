One EnergyPlus reference + climate and ISTAT transfer

36 scenarios · 100 m² · reference: Milano__Multi-family__A2

The three methods, simply

  1. EnergyPlus: build and simulate a virtual home for each case.
  2. Hybrid: simulate one reference home. Learn its relationship between daily weather and demand. Shift that relationship using ISTAT household gains and operating hours, and scale it for the target exposure. Sum target weather features to obtain annual energy. No target heat-balance simulation. The earlier ISTAT-ratio baseline remains available.
  3. ISTAT only: report what the survey analysis supports: heating/cooling hours and reconstructed appliance electricity. Thermal kWh remain blank.

Transfer process

E(target,u) = E_EP(reference,u) × H(target)/H(reference) × D(target,u)/D(reference,u)

H is nominal exposed-envelope plus ventilation/ground conductance. D sums a fitted weather index, adjusted for ISTAT-derived mean controls and gains. Balance temperatures and solar coefficients use only reference EP daily totals. The baseline still uses direct ISTAT-index ratios.

  1. Fit supported survey groups on training households. Pool predictions within each region × dwelling × archetype cohort; cooling uses valid reported hours.
  2. Run or reuse the reference EnergyPlus case once. Fit the reference weather signature.
  3. Calculate exposure, operating-hours and gains adjustments from configured geometry and supported ISTAT profiles. Save predictions and sequential component checks before any target simulations.
  4. Optionally run target EnergyPlus cases to assess error, excluding the reference. Never feed these results back into predictions.

ISTAT hours constrain assumed mean thermostat settings, rather than multiplying degree days by another regional hours ratio. Exposure, gain utilisation and fixed schedule assumptions still need validation. No hybrid hourly/monthly load shape is provided. Missing inputs and insufficient reference heating/cooling activity return unavailable estimates.

Assumptions and limitations

  • One fixed-area hypothetical space; not a surveyed home or a metered validation case.
  • The baseline scales reference energy by survey hours. The extended hybrid learns its balance point and solar response from reference daily EP energy only.
  • Survey indices use training households, supported joint cohorts and survey weights. Missing cooling hours are excluded, not filled.
  • Cooling hours describe reporting cooling users; they are not an unconditional population cooling-energy index.
  • Target EPW weather supplies daily climate and solar indices; survey macro-regions supply household associations, not exact city observations.
  • Exposure and mean internal-gain adjustments are static assumptions. The hybrid has no thermal capacitance, indoor-temperature state or target thermal simulation.
  • Hybrid annual estimates have no inferred hourly shape, peak load or monthly distribution.
  • Detailed EP models use assumed daily schedules, adiabatic neighbours and constant ground temperature.
  • Appliance input and transfer use the same survey reconstruction; agreement is not independent validation.
  • Data-only reports hours and reconstructed appliance kWh; thermal kWh remain unavailable.
